About

I am a Senior Research Engineer at Fauna Robotics, working on computer vision, robotics perception, and embodied AI for robots that operate in real homes. My work focuses on helping robots perceive and understand the people, objects, and spaces around them, and on deploying machine learning systems that run reliably on-device in real time.

Previously, I was a Senior Software Engineer at Amazon, where I worked on perception for Amazon Astro from 2018 to 2026. I have over eight years of experience building production computer vision and machine learning systems in C++ and Python.
